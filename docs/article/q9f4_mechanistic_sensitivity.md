# Q-9F.4 - Targeted mechanistic sensitivity screening

Q-9F.4 performs a centred one-at-a-time screening of height growth, DBH growth, diffusion and mortality. Each scientific coefficient is multiplied by 0.8 and 1.2 while the reconstructed 1992 state, numerical scheme, grid and all other coefficients remain fixed.

The eight trajectories are compared with reconstructed states at 1998, 2016 and 2020 using stand summaries and Q-9F.3 distribution distances. Centred finite-difference elasticities describe local signatures of the four mechanisms. They are not calibrated values, likelihood-based estimates or proof that one mechanism causes the empirical discrepancy.

The workflow is independent of the provisional height-provider implementation after native states have been generated. Replacing upstream heights regenerates the initial state and targets without changing the sensitivity design.
