# Q-9F.2 - Main trajectory from 1992 to 2020

Q-9F.2 starts from the reconstructed native 1992 state at GIS Coop age 45 and advances the unchanged article dynamics to age 73. It saves comparisons at 1992/45, 1998/51, 2016/69 and 2020/73. Targets are the independently reconstructed native states from Q-9E.5, not a recalibration dataset.

The comparison reports N, G, mean and quadratic DBH, DBH dispersion, mean and quadratic height, dominant height, height-DBH correlation, dominant-minus-mean height and cumulative stem loss. The 1992 row is an identity check. Later residuals combine dynamic-model discrepancy, provisional height reconstruction and numerical representation. No parameter is adjusted in this lot.

The upstream height provider remains replaceable. Rebuilding Q-9E.3 to Q-9E.5 with observed or institutional heights is sufficient to regenerate the targets and initial state without changing the Q-9F.2 dynamic contract.
