# Q-9F.5 - Hierarchical diagnostics of vertical structure

Q-9F.5 tests whether the vertical discrepancy identified in Q-9F.2 and Q-9F.3 is concentrated in suppressed, subordinate, co-dominant or dominant parts of the stand. Each native state is partitioned using state-specific relative DBH `D/Dg` classes: below 0.60, 0.60-0.80, 0.80-1.00, 1.00-1.20, 1.20-1.50 and at least 1.50.

For each class, the workflow reports density, density share, mean DBH, mean and quadratic height, mean slenderness, contribution to stand mean height, and contribution to the second height moment. Simulated and reconstructed snapshots are compared in 1998, 2016 and 2020; 1992 is retained as an identity reference.

The analysis is Eulerian. It does not claim to track individual trees through the continuous dynamic state and therefore does not call snapshot differences individual height growth. Height targets remain provisional and replaceable. No dynamic or allometric parameter is adjusted.
