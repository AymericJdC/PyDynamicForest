# Q-9F.7 diagnostic completion - mass balance

The status-shape experiment modifies only the height-transport term within a given step, but its changed state feeds back into the native status used by DBH transport and mortality at subsequent steps. It is therefore a pathway intervention, not a guarantee that only height observables change over a full trajectory.

The diagnostic records realised density change, the next-step difference from a baseline step started from the exact same state, baseline and transformed height-transport integrals, baseline DBH-transport integral, instantaneous mortality-sink integral, diffusion range, status bounds, density extrema and the first cumulative-mass alert.

Because the production step is implicit and couples transport, diffusion and mortality, instantaneous integrals are descriptive and are not asserted to add exactly to realised mass change. Q-9F.7 remains unvalidated until this diagnostic is interpreted. No alternative gamma is adopted.
