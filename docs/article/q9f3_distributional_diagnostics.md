# Q-9F.3 - Distributional diagnostics without calibration

Q-9F.3 compares simulated and independently reconstructed native states at 1998, 2016 and 2020 on the common PyDynamicForest grid. Native nodal masses are used, not raw density values.

For DBH and height marginals it reports weighted quantiles, means, standard deviations, first Wasserstein distance, maximum CDF distance and integrated CDF distance. Joint diagnostics include height-DBH correlation, slenderness distribution, dominant-minus-mean height, and positive/negative cell-mass discrepancies.

The diameter targets are directly grounded in individual inventories. Height targets remain provisional reconstructions from the replaceable provider. Cell-mass discrepancies localise net structural mismatch but are not labelled as observed mortality, because snapshots alone do not identify the process generating each difference. No calibration is performed.
